## Clock qualification

A fresh, version-specific observer brackets the original chrono calls with
`CLOCK_MONOTONIC_RAW`, retaining the original native return and fields. It
changes no GPU kernel or scheduler value. Separate four-capture scopes were
used for 8K and 16K. Each captures the published prefill interval and the decode
interval whose generated counter moves 1→128; the rate numerator 128 follows
Halogen's existing convention, while the observed delta 127 is recorded
separately. Instrumentation overhead remains included.

All eight complete RAW upper phase-sum brackets fit their matching full HTTP
duration measured with Windows QPC. Residuals including warmups are 14.03–55.77 ms
at 8K and 81.52–98.32 ms at 16K. Whole-request measured means are 6.648199 s and
11.392420s. Observer journal writes occur after the final phase stamp and are
included in HTTP time. Collector response-file persistence is excluded from
the HTTP timestamp.

Native/API phase durations agree with the direct capture in these cohorts.
The earlier [API-only clock counterexamples](halogen01822-natural-8k16k-20261101.md)
remain unchanged: agreement here does not establish a universal correction or
prove why those earlier clocks disagreed. No multiplier, overhead subtraction
or inferred phase duration was used. Generic HTTP-client summary metadata is
conservative; qualification comes from the separate RAW reader and paired
QPC audit retained in the [JSON report](halogen01822-raw-8k16k-20261101.json).

Per-run decode rates range 49.96–57.82 tok/s at 8K and 44.55–49.55 at 16K.
The headline uses all three measurements, not the fastest sample.

measure internal GPU chunk counts.

The separate staged two-request integration has now completed its
[mixed 8K+16K comparison](halogen01822-concurrency2-20261101.md). It improves
whole-pair completed-output throughput by 5.70–5.77%, while increasing individual
request latency. Normal serving retains one slot. These are independent
answers, rather than parallel MTP heads for one answer; the aggregate result
does not replace the single-request phase rates above. No NPU speedup or
full-goal completion is claimed.
