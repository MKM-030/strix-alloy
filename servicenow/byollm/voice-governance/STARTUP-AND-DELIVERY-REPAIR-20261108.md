## What changed

1. **The configured introduction overlaps media setup.** After caller authorization, voice preparation and validation of the remote offer, the SDK starts the existing owned conversation worker while ICE gathers. Previously that worker waited until gathering finished. The same worker prepares the introduction and native dialog; it is not started twice. Audio stays buffered until the peer connects. Failed negotiation, timeout or deletion closes and drains that owner, including its pending native bootstrap. The shared SDK adapter serves iPhone and Android; the browser already starts its worker before negotiating media.
2. **Voice languages prepare concurrently.** The selected ServiceNow voice references are prepared with at most four concurrent requests, rather than serial network waits. Every locale still authorizes under the current caller, including cache hits. Registration remains serialized for an identical immutable voice alias. Failed or cancelled admission drains all requests and publishes no partial language map. Both SDK and browser use this common preparation path.
3. **Conversation receives the actual current input's delivery state.** A locally queued correction is no longer described as an already transmitted request simply because the conversation is busy. The prompt distinguishes local waiting, a held input, a handoff attempt, an owned native reply and uncertain delivery. It binds evidence to the caller turn, native owner and request. An older reply cannot establish the status of a newer correction. Transport acknowledgment is deliberately not proof of business processing and does not invalidate an otherwise current rapid response.
4. **Unsolicited processing narration is discouraged.** The optional model is instructed to respond to content and corrections, and to mention transmission or missing confirmation only when relevant. Duplicate generic waiting instructions were removed. The observed invented causal claim that information was withheld because business fields were missing is rejected during bootstrap as well as during ordinary pending work. This narrow language guard is not a general semantic correctness guarantee.
5. **Setup durations are observable.** Numeric-only logs now distinguish admission, locale preparation, call allocation and media readiness. No credentials or reference audio are logged.
6. **Obsolete optional work relinquishes the model queue before first text.** A meaningful native-context change cancels and drains only the exact obsolete optional response before its first protocol text. The reusable conversation survives. Ownership fences cover context updates and scheduled turn dispatch. Acknowledgments, passive sounds and progress counters do not cancel. Begun text retains its source lease; native business execution is untouched. Three new offline regressions and 51 related checks passed, with an independent review.
